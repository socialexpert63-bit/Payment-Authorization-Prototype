How an engagement runs

Discovery. We agree the question: approval dips, timeouts, or a specific incident.
Data review. Anonymized samples are traced across stages to locate where failures cluster.
Assessment. Possible causes are listed as hypotheses, then tested against evidence.
Recommendations. You receive prioritized actions and a report your team can act on.
